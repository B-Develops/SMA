Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order? Browse the car listings, click on a car you like, enter your offer amount on the listing page, and click "Place Order". Your order will be sent to the seller for confirmation.
What happens after I place an order? You'll see your order in "My Orders" on your dashboard. The seller will review and either confirm or cancel the order. You'll receive a notification when your order is confirmed.
What payment methods are accepted? We accept bank transfers, USSD payments, and cash on delivery (for local pickup). Confirmed orders receive detailed payment instructions via your dashboard.
Can I inspect the car before buying? Yes! You can request an inspection or visit the seller's location. We highly recommend inspecting the car in person before completing your purchase.
What if I have issues with my purchase? Contact our support team immediately. We can help mediate disputes and ensure you have a smooth transaction.
How do I save a car for later? Click the heart icon on any listing to save it to your Watchlist. You can view all saved cars on your dashboard.
Where is Sarkin Mota Autos located? We operate across Nigeria. Our main office is in Abuja. We facilitate car sales throughout the country with delivery services available.
Who is the founder of Sarkin Mota Autos? Sarkin Mota Autos was founded by Dr. Aliyu Mohammad (Sarkin Mota), a trusted name in Nigeria's automotive industry.
