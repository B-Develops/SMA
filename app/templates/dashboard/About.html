About Sarkin Mota Autos

Sarkin Mota Autos is a trusted Nigerian car dealership built on transparency, quality vehicles, and nationwide delivery.

AM

Dr. Aliyu Mohammad (Al-Amin)

Founder & CEO

Sarkin Mota Autos was founded in 2019 by Dr. Aliyu Mohammad, widely known as Sarkin Mota — “King of Cars”. Based in Abuja, the business grew through trust, consistency, and digital innovation to serve customers across Nigeria.

Our Story

What started as a physical car dealership in Abuja evolved into a nationwide online platform during the COVID-19 period. Through transparent listings, verified vehicles, and social media engagement, Sarkin Mota Autos continues to help Nigerians buy cars with confidence.
