Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy applies to Sarkin Mota Autos ("we", "us", "our"). It does not constitute legal advice — have it reviewed by qualified counsel for your jurisdiction.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, and a securely hashed password (bcrypt).
  • Listing data: car details you submit (make, model, year, price, mileage, description) and uploaded images.
  • Order data: order amounts, payment method, and delivery address you provide.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and device information via server logs and analytics.
  • Communications: any messages or support requests you send us.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your email.
  • To list, display, and match your car advertisements.
  • To process and confirm orders and facilitate delivery.
  • To send service-related notifications (order confirmations, delivery updates, security alerts).
  • To improve the platform, diagnose problems, and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. Legal basis (NG)

We process data under Nigeria's Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA): contractual necessity (account/orders), your consent (marketing), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, analytics).

4. Cookies and tracking

We use essential session cookies (to keep you logged in) and security cookies (CSRF). Optional analytics cookies may be set by third parties (e.g., Google Fonts). You may disable cookies in your browser, but this may limit your ability to log in or place orders.

5. Data retention

  • Active accounts: retained while active or as needed.
  • Deleted/anonymized accounts: retained in anonymized form indefinitely (no personal identifiers).
  • Order and payment records: retained for as long as necessary for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution (minimum 7 years where required by law).
  • Server logs (IPs): retained for a maximum of 90 days.

6. Your rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us (see "Contact" below). Note that deleting your account anonymizes your data rather than permanently erasing it, where retention is required by law.

7. International transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in Nigeria and other jurisdictions. We rely on lawful mechanisms (e.g., consent, necessity for contract) for any such transfers.

8. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures: bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS (HSTS + TLS), CSRF protection, secure session cookies, rate limiting, and regular backups. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third parties

We may share data with service providers (hosting, email, payments, analytics) who are bound to confidentiality, and as required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

10. Children

Our service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

Questions? Contact us at privacy@sarkinmotaautos.com.
