Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and web beacons.

2. How we use cookies

Sarkin Mota Autos uses cookies to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep you logged in (essential/session);
  • Protect the platform against fraud and abuse (security);
  • Remember your preferences (e.g., theme, notification settings);
  • Analyze how visitors use the site so we can improve performance (analytics).

3. Types of cookies we use

4. Third-party cookies

We may load resources (such as Google Fonts) from third parties that can set their own cookies. We are not responsible for those parties' cookies. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or behavioral tracking.

5. Managing your cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may prevent you from logging in or placing orders. To opt out of analytics cookies, adjust your notification settings under Settings.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
